## 4. Cold Outbound Field Sales & Trade Association Playbook

Moroccan merchants buy on trust, demonstration, and immediate peer proof. The most effective scaling engine is the **Field Merchant Ambassador Model**:

1. **The "Live Mobile Demo" Bag:**
   - Sales reps carry a lightweight portable backpack with a tablet running KASPI POS, a Bluetooth 58mm thermal printer, and a PAX A920 demo terminal.
2. **The 3-Minute Walk-In Script:**
   - *"السلام عليكم سي الحاج! حنا شركة مغربية سميتنا كسب. ما جيناش نبيعو ليك بي سي كبير ياخد ليك البلاصة فالمحل. جينا نوريوك كيفاش تضبط الكريدي والسلعة ديالك فـ 3 دقايق ومن التيليفون ديالك."*
3. **The On-the-Spot Printed Ticket:**
   - The ambassador types the merchant's store name into KASPI POS, scans a product on the merchant's own shelf, and prints a real thermal receipt with the merchant's own name on it in 5 seconds.
   - Handing the physical printed ticket with their own store name converts over 40% of cold walk-in merchants on the spot.
4. **Zero-Friction Onboarding:**
   - No hardware lock-in. Works on the merchant's existing laptop, tablet, or smartphone.
   - 14-day free trial with direct personal WhatsApp onboarding support.
