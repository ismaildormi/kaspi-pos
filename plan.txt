--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Language & Regional Compliance: Native Arabic (RTL default), French, English;

  - 1-click full export in CSV, JSON, and SQLite formats.
  - Builds radical merchant trust and satisfies Moroccan CNDP data sovereignty.

[MOAT 3] Secondary Revenue: Anonymized Market Business Intelligence (B2B):
  - Opt-in anonymized aggregation of purchasing trends (e.g., top selling FMCG 
    in Casablanca vs Marrakech, price elasticity, supply chain demand).
  - High-margin B2B data subscriptions for distributors and brand manufacturers.

[MOAT 4] Infrastructure Cost Optimization ($0/mo Floor):
  - Production deployment optimized for Oracle Cloud Always Free Tier:
    4 ARM Ampere Cores, 24GB RAM, 200GB NVMe Storage.
  - Next.js Standalone container + Alpine Linux + PostgreSQL 16 connection pooling.
  - Generates 95%+ gross profit margins on $10/month subscription pricing.
